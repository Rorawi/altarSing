# AltarSing backlog

Open follow-ups gathered from the product feedback so far. The current service-log spacing adjustment is implemented locally and is not listed as outstanding.

## Voice Studio

- [ ] Investigate the reported tempo drift while recording a new part over the metronome and previously recorded backing parts. Reproduce with the same tempo and compare the backing playback speed with the saved take.
- [ ] Verify first-tap playback in the installed iPhone PWA: “Listen to all parts” should start without first playing an individual take, and existing parts should play while recording a new one. Confirm on-device after the audio startup changes.
- [ ] Review recording retention and cleanup so expired projects and their stored audio are removed predictably, with storage usage visible enough to manage costs.

## Song organization

- [ ] Revisit how music directors capture, organize, and retrieve song ideas across the Library, collections, rehearsals, and service history. The current flow can feel difficult when collecting songs and ideas.

## Before onboarding other choirs

- [ ] Design private choir workspaces with music-director accounts and strict choir-level data access. Keep singer listening simple through revocable, expiring share links where full accounts are unnecessary.
- [ ] Add storage and playback usage monitoring, then choose hosting limits based on a small choir pilot before broad promotion.
